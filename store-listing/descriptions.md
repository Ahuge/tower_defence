## Full description (max 4000 chars)

```
Build the maze. Break the wave.

Factions is a deep tower defense game where every tower you place bends the creep path a little further, and every empty square between them is a decision waiting to happen. Twelve asymmetric factions — Arcane, Mechanical, Nature, Void, Military, Spawn Aliens, Cypherpunk, Infernal, Celestial, Psionic, Harmonic, and Random — each play completely differently. Stack burns with Mechanical, poison with Nature, gamble for gold with Void, or build a conduit network with Harmonic.

▸ EIGHT GAME MODES
• Standard — classic 15/30/100-wave tower defense
• Endless — scaling difficulty, plays until you break
• Battle — dual economy with gold and compounding essence
• Hero Defense — 30 waves, 11 unlockable heroes, ultimate abilities, item shop
• Faction Gauntlet — 100 waves across 10 homeworld maps, each stage a different enemy faction
• Versus 1v1 — peer-to-peer multiplayer, sends go to your opponent
• Circle Co-op — 2-4 players share one circular map

▸ 80+ UNIQUE TOWERS
Every faction has its own roster of 5-8 towers plus a signature Ultimate. Towers aren't just damage numbers — Infernal towers expire after a few waves, Celestial towers gain lives and absorb leaks, Psionic towers ignore armor entirely, Military units leave their base and move to engage the creeps.

▸ ECONOMY TRIANGLE
Spend gold on towers (direct defense), sends (spawn extra creeps on your own wave for permanent income bonus), or Frontier buildings (safe passive income). Each faction's Frontier works differently — Mechanical digs for more gold with collapse risk, Nature grows and harvests on a cycle, Void gambles for jackpots, Arcane's Leyline Nexus has an on-demand 3× gold burst.

▸ PEER-TO-PEER MULTIPLAYER
Versus 1v1 sends go to your opponent as extra creeps, forcing them to defend against what you build. Circle Co-op shares one map between 2-4 players with zone-restricted building and shared lives. No servers, no sign-ups, just clipboard-based WebRTC match-start.

▸ DEEP STRATEGY, HAND-TUNED DIFFICULTY
Easy / Normal / Hard / Insane difficulty tiers don't just bump hit points — each creep type interprets difficulty individually. Armored creeps get tankier, swarms multiply, fast creeps get faster. Insane mode stacks extra traits: 45% evasion, boss damage-cap shields, armored regen. Probably not winnable.

▸ UNLOCKABLE COSMETICS
12 faction skin packs, 11 hero skins, per-tower rolls, and terrain themes. Earn shards by winning matches or buy them directly. A battle pass tracks per-season progress with free and premium tiers.

▸ PLAYS THE WAY YOU DO
• Tablet and phone responsive layouts with pinch-to-zoom camera
• TAB / speed button cycles between 0x, 0.5x, 1x, 1.5x, and 2x (with 3x unlocked via Battle Pass)
• Pause anytime. The wave won't judge you.
• Full tutorial at first launch. Faction primers fire the first time you try each one.
• Daily +100 shard reward for opening the Store.

▸ REMOVE ADS
The optional one-time "Remove Ads" upgrade disables all interstitials and banners forever. Rewarded ads remain available as an optional bonus path — watch one to claim shards or revive a lost match if you want to.

Built by Running Man Games. Zero loot boxes, zero pay-to-win. The only things purchasable are cosmetics, the shard currency used to roll for cosmetics, and the ad-free upgrade. Every piece of gameplay content is earnable through play.
```
