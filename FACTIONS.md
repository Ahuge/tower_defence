# Factions

## Arcane (7 towers)
*Precision magic. Crits, AoE, and spell amplification.*

| Tower | Cost | Type | Key Traits |
|-------|------|------|------------|
| Bolt | 25g | Magic DPS | Direct damage. 4 upgrade levels — the workhorse. |
| Frost | 35g | CC | 65% slow for 2.5s. No upgrades — pure utility. |
| Storm | 55g | AoE | Splash damage. Good against packs. |
| Focus | 90g | Sniper | Long range. 25% crit chance for 3x damage. |
| Mana Drain | 120g | Utility | Strips boss shields on hit. Essential for late-game bosses. |
| Meteor | 200g | Heavy AoE | Slow-falling projectile, massive splash on impact. |
| **Arcane Nova** | **700g** | **Ultimate** | AoE + crit + slow + damage amp. Everything Arcane does, combined. |

**Identity:** Arcane excels at magic damage and crowd control. Frost + Storm handles packs, Focus picks off high-value targets, Mana Drain is the anti-boss answer. Nova is the endgame capstone.

**Frontier:** Leyline Nexus (overcharge for burst gold, dormancy risk).

## Mechanical (8 towers)
*Engineering and firepower. Burn, pierce, and raw efficiency.*

| Tower | Cost | Type | Key Traits |
|-------|------|------|------------|
| Wall | 10g | Blocker | Dirt cheap maze filler. No upgrades. |
| Turret | 30g | Ramp DPS | Fire rate increases on same target. 4 upgrade levels. |
| Flamethrower | 40g | Burn AoE | Short range splash + burn DoT (8 DPS for 3s). |
| Tesla | 80g | Chain | Chain lightning jumps to 3+ targets. |
| Mortar | 120g | Artillery | Extreme range (8+ tiles), huge splash. |
| Shredder | 150g | Debuff | Very fast fire. Shreds armor tier for 4s per hit. |
| Railgun | 300g | Pierce | Damages ALL creeps in a line. Corridor destroyer. |
| **Titan Cannon** | **800g** | **Ultimate** | 500 damage, range 12, splash + burn + armor shred. |

**Identity:** Mechanical has the widest cost range and most towers. Wall enables complex mazes cheaply. Turret rewards patience. Flamethrower + Shredder combo strips armor and applies DoT. Railgun devastates corridors. Titan Cannon is the ultimate artillery.

**Frontier:** Deep Mine (dig deeper for +income, cave-in risk).

## Nature (6 towers)
*Growth and synergy. Poison, roots, and adjacency buffs.*

| Tower | Cost | Type | Key Traits |
|-------|------|------|------------|
| Thorn | 20g | Physical DPS | Cheap, reliable. 5 upgrade levels — scales all game. |
| Root | 35g | CC | Strongest slow in game: 70% for 3s. |
| Blossom | 60g | Support | Buffs adjacent towers: +15% damage, +8% fire rate per level. |
| Spore | 100g | Area Denial | Poisons ALL creeps near the tower (not the target). 2% max HP/s. |
| Vine | 160g | Stun | 20% chance to fully root (stun) creeps for 0.8s. |
| **Elder Treant** | **600g** | **Ultimate** | Grows +8% damage permanently. Roots enemies. Buffs adjacents. |

**Identity:** Nature is about synergy and scaling. Blossom amplifies everything around it. Thorn with 5 upgrades + Blossom buffs is a DPS machine. Spore provides area denial (place on chokepoints). Elder Treant is the ultimate synergy piece — it grows, roots, and buffs.

**Frontier:** Sacred Grove (grows income over waves, harvest for burst payout).

## Void (5 towers)
*Pure chaos. Gambling, gold generation, and teleportation.*

| Tower | Cost | Type | Key Traits |
|-------|------|------|------------|
| Gambler | 15g | Chaos | 8% instant kill, 25% zero damage. Cheapest tower. |
| Spike | 30g | Variance DPS | Each shot deals 50-150% damage randomly. |
| Siphon | 50g | Economy | +1g per hit. 4 upgrade levels — the gold engine. |
| Rift | 120g | Displacement | Teleports creeps backward on their path. |
| **Oblivion** | **900g** | **Ultimate** | 15% instakill, +3g/hit, extreme variance, damage amp. |

**Identity:** Void is high-risk, high-reward. Gambler at 15g is the cheapest tower in the game but unreliable. Siphon funds your economy. Rift is unique displacement. Oblivion is the most expensive tower in the game but does everything chaotically.

**Frontier:** The Rift (random income 0-15g or 0-30g per wave — pure gamble).

## Random (dynamic)
*6 random towers each wave from all factions. Adapt or die.*

Each wave, 6 non-ultimate towers are randomly chosen from all faction pools. Bought towers stay on the field permanently, but may not be available to purchase again for several waves. Forces adaptive strategy — you build with whatever the game gives you.

No faction-specific frontier buildings (uses generic outposts).
